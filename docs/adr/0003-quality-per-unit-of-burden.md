# Optimize verified quality per unit of burden

C.A.S.E. adds process only when its expected quality or safety gain exceeds its human, context, latency, and maintenance cost. MECE-Autopilot remains prior art and an optional companion; C.A.S.E. may borrow a bounded `decision-challenge` policy, but simulated roles, mandatory rounds, another state machine, and broad automatic triggering do not enter the core.
